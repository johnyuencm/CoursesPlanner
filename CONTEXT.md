# MSCS Seattle course planning

The planner relates the official program requirements to cataloged courses and a student's plan.

## Language

**Program map**:
The view of cataloged courses listed in the MSCS Seattle requirements together with their cataloged direct prerequisites and corequisites, including courses with no relationships.

**Neighborhood**:
Courses connected to a focus course within the chosen relationship distance, or throughout its connected graph when all connections are requested.

**Selected chain**:
The selected course together with its upstream prerequisites and downstream connections. It determines which relationships the program map emphasizes.

**Map projection**:
The courses, relationships, and positions shown for a chosen map scope, focus course, and selected course.
