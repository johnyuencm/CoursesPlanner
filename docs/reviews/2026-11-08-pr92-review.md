## What I verified

- **v1 refactor.** I put the master copy of `lib/validation.ts` next to the PR copy. I ran both on 24,000 seeded plans. The `DegreeProgress` results were deep-equal for every plan.
- **Catalog variants in that sweep.** (1) real MSCS Seattle. (2) A mutated catalog: one breadth course in three areas, one in two areas, a core course in a breadth area, a core course in electives, a breadth course in electives, variable credits (2 to 4) and 3-credit courses. (3) The mutated catalog with stricter breadth (4 courses, 3 areas, 14 credits). The plans had duplicates, waivers, planned courses and credit selections. 581 plans were fully satisfied and 1,655 had breadth satisfied, so the sweep is not vacuous.
- **v1 against v2.** The same 24,000 plans also went through `tests/v2-equivalence.ts`. All matched.
- **Equivalence wrapper.** It compares v1 against a projection of v2 (not v1 against itself). It uses `assert.deepEqual` on the full `DegreeProgress`, so a missing or extra field also fails.
- **Mutation checks.** Each of these mutations in `lib/requirements-v2.ts` makes the suite fail: electives list without breadth courses (2 fail), waivers ignored (3 fail), `minAreas` set to 0 (3 fail).
- **Determinism.** `chooseBreadth` sorts by code. `choose-courses` follows the group's list order. `choose-credits` sums every eligible course, so its credit total does not depend on plan order. Only the order of the `courses` array follows plan order.
- `unmodeled` is never satisfied. An `all` group that holds one is never satisfied.
